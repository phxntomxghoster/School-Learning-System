ORION OS Legal Compliance Architecture

To keep your school project safe from copyright strikes or liability issues, Project ORION OS uses a Legal Emulator & Homebrew Hub architecture:

  • No Copyrighted BIOS Files: The system does not bundle or distribute proprietary system BIOS or copyrighted commercial ROMs.
  • Homebrew & Open Source Support: The vault allows users to load their own permitted indie homebrew games, open-source engine ports, or web-standard WASM binaries.
  • Client-Side Processing: All files selected by users run locally inside their browser's secure memory space via HTML5/JavaScript/WASM.
