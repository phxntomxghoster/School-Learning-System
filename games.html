The Impact of the Industrial Revolution

The Industrial Revolution, which began in Britain in the late 18th century, marked a major turning point in history. Almost every aspect of daily life was influenced in some way. In particular, average income and population began to exhibit unprecedented sustained growth. Some economists say that the major impact of the Industrial Revolution was that the standard of living for the general population began to increase consistently for the first time in history.
